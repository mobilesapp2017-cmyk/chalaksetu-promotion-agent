# ChalakSetu AI Promotion

**Date:** 2026-08-25

**Target:** Vehicle owners and employers looking for drivers

**Creative Style:** Question hook

**Topic:** Finding drivers for commercial or personal vehicles quickly

**Marketing Angle:** Direct and utility-focused

## Reel Details

**Duration:** 8-10 seconds

**Hook:** Driver chahiye, par sahi profile kaise dhoondhen?

### Scene 1 (0-2 seconds)

**Visual:** A fleet owner standing in a transport yard, looking at his line of commercial trucks with a thoughtful expression.

**Voiceover:** Driver chahiye, par sahi profile kahan milegi?

### Scene 2 (2-5 seconds)

**Visual:** A close-up shot of hands scrolling through a clean list of driver profiles on a smartphone.

**Voiceover:** Ab car, bus ya truck ke liye...

### Scene 3 (5-8 seconds)

**Visual:** The owner taps on a profile to connect with a potential driver candidate.

**Voiceover:** ...available profiles search karna hua bilkul asaan.

### Scene 4 (8-10 seconds)

**Visual:** The owner shakes hands with a driver, panning to the ChalakSetu brand name and chalaksetu.in.

**Voiceover:** Visit karo chalaksetu.in!

## Hindi Script

Driver chahiye? Pareshani chodo! ChalakSetu par car, bus, truck ya taxi drivers ke liye available profiles search karo. Visit chalaksetu.in!
